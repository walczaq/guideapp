Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 August 2026

Fieldnote is a live walking-tour companion: a guide runs a tour session, and passengers join it to see the tour map, revealed stops, and each other's position during the tour. This policy explains what data Fieldnote handles, why, and what happens to it. It applies to the Fieldnote web app at fieldnote.guide and the Fieldnote mobile apps, which are the same application.

What we collect, and why

  • Your live location — only during a tour. If you allow location access, your position is shared with your guide and shown on the tour's shared map while the tour session is running. In the mobile app this continues with the screen off — so your guide can see you even with your phone in your pocket — and a visible notification is shown the whole time. It turns off by itself when the tour ends, after 12 hours at the latest, or whenever you switch it off (menu → "Stop sharing my location"). Our server additionally refuses location data for ended tours. We never track your location outside a tour session. In the browser version, location is shared only while the page is open.
  • The name you enter when joining. Shown to your guide and tour group so they can recognize you on the map. Use any name you like.
  • Push notification tokens. If you enable notifications, we store the delivery address your browser or phone issues for this purpose (a web-push subscription or a device push token) so we can notify you when a stop starts, a warning is posted, or it's time to head back. Disabling notifications removes it.
  • Group chat messages and photos. If your tour uses the group chat, the messages and photos you post there are visible to the tour group and stored with the session.
  • Language and appearance preferences, stored on your own device.
  • For guides: an account name, sign-in credentials, and the tours you build.

What we do NOT do

  • No selling of personal data — to anyone, ever.
  • No advertising, no ad trackers, no third-party analytics profiles.
  • No location tracking outside a live tour session — ever. During a tour, the mobile app's in-your-pocket sharing always shows a visible notification and always shuts itself off when the tour ends.
  • No collection beyond what the tour needs to run.

Who your data is shared with

Your location, name, and chat activity are visible to your guide and — on the shared map — to the rest of your tour group, only during the tour. Beyond that, data is handled by the infrastructure providers Fieldnote runs on, acting as processors:

  • Supabase (database & realtime, hosted in the EU — Frankfurt) — stores sessions, locations, names, chat, and push tokens.
  • Cloudflare — serves the app itself.
  • Mapbox — serves the map tiles (your device requests tiles directly, which involves your IP address, like any website).
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service — deliver push notifications to the mobile apps, if you enable them.

Retention

Tour sessions end automatically. Session data (including location history and chat) is retained afterwards so tour operators can review how tours went and so we can improve Fieldnote, and is periodically cleaned up. Push tokens are removed when they stop working or when you disable notifications. If you want data about you deleted, email us — we'll do it promptly.

Your choices

  • Location and notification permissions are optional — the app works without them; you'll simply follow along on the map without appearing on it or being notified.
  • Both can be turned off any time in the menu or your device settings.
  • For access, correction, or deletion of your data, contact us below.

Children

Fieldnote is used by tour groups of all ages under the supervision of a tour guide. We do not knowingly collect data from children beyond what any passenger's participation in a tour involves, and we collect no more from anyone than the tour needs.

Changes

If Fieldnote's data handling changes, this policy is updated first and the date above changes. (Most recent change: described the mobile app's in-your-pocket location sharing during tours, with its visible notification and automatic shut-off.)

Contact
